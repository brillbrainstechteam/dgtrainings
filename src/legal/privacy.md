## 1. Introduction

DG Trainings is a professional training offering operated by
**[BrillBrains Consultants Pvt. Ltd.](https://www.brillbrainsconsultants.com/)** ("BrillBrains", "we", "our" or
"us") under the DG Trainings brand.

We provide professional and business training programs for individuals,
teams, managers, business leaders, and organizations, with a particular
focus on the jewellery industry.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and
protect personal information when you visit our website, contact us,
submit an enquiry, request a training appointment, book a training
program, participate in training, or otherwise interact with us.

**Website:** https://dgtrainings.com/

information.

The specific information collected depends on the website configuration
and the services enabled at the time of your visit.

Where applicable, we may maintain billing names, business details,
invoices, transaction references, and payment status. Payment
credentials should be submitted only through appropriate payment
channels and not through ordinary enquiry forms.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use information for legitimate business purposes, including:

-   Responding to enquiries and arranging appointments.
-   Preparing proposals and customized training programs.
-   Delivering, administering, and evaluating training sessions.
-   Coordinating with clients, authorized representatives, and
    participants.
-   Sending schedules, reminders, joining instructions, materials, and
    follow-up communications.
-   Maintaining attendance, feedback, assessments, and service records.
-   Processing payments, issuing invoices, and maintaining accounting
    records.
-   Understanding website traffic and improving website functionality
    and user experience.
-   Publishing authorized testimonials, client references, photographs,
    and promotional content.
-   Sending relevant business updates or marketing communications where
    permitted.
-   Meeting applicable legal, regulatory, contractual, and security
    requirements.

We will not use personal information for unrelated purposes without an
appropriate legal basis.

## 4. Cookies and Analytics

The DG Trainings website has no user sign-up or login facility. Visitors
may submit an enquiry through the website form. We may use essential
cookies or similar technologies needed for website operation, security,
form functionality, or to remember necessary preferences. We may also
use website analytics to understand traffic and improve the website.

Cookies and analytics technologies, where enabled, may collect technical
information such as pages viewed, session activity, browser and device
details, referral sources, and general usage patterns. We do not
intentionally collect login credentials through this website because it
does not offer user accounts.

The exact analytics or cookie providers may change as the website is
updated. We will identify relevant third-party providers in this Policy
or through appropriate website notices once confirmed from the deployed
website configuration.

Where required by applicable law, we will provide appropriate notice and
obtain consent before using non-essential cookies or tracking
technologies. Visitors should be provided with a practical means to
manage or withdraw consent where required.

You may also manage certain cookies through your browser settings.
Disabling cookies may affect some website features.
