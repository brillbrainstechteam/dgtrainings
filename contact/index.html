FAQs

Questions, answered

Quick answers before you call. Anything else, ask us on WhatsApp.

Are the programs customised for my business?

Yes. Every program is tailored to your products, team size, roles and goals — whether you are a retailer, wholesaler or manufacturer.

How long does a program run?

From focused half-day workshops to multi-session programs over several months, depending on your goals. Confirm typical durations.

Can we combine several programs?

Yes. Many teams pair programs — for example Orientation for new joiners with Business & Technical – Sales Expert Training for the floor team.

Which languages do you train in?

e.g., English, Hindi, Gujarati, Marathi — confirm languages offered.

Where is the training delivered?

On-site at your showroom, office or factory; at a venue of your choice; in the field during sales tours and exhibitions.
